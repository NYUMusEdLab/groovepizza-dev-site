Their matching MIT, 0BSD and MIT/Zlib notices are now retained in notices/release-components and copied into the public artifact; the JSZip MIT option is selected. This resolves the identified embedded-component notice gap. It does not clear the inherited media or brand permissions.

The new MIDI encoder, MusicXML writer, Scratch project generator, browser store, Notebook UI, and Blob-download helper were written for this task.
