## Drum banks, presets and integrations

Fifteen WAV files in five drum banks are present: afro-latin, hip-hop, jazz,
rock and techno. `app/data/kits.json` links these banks; runtime configuration
can also load remote content and custom samples. Record the sample sources,
recording/performer permissions and allowed uses before redistributing them.

`app/data/grooves.json` contains named musical presets, including Billie Jean
and Chameleon. Separate rights in any protected musical content from the
software implementation; this record makes no finding about whether an
individual rhythmic pattern is protected or already authorized.

Images include partner/social marks and external social-preview images. A
software license does not grant use of Noteflight, Soundtrap, Facebook or
Twitter branding, or broaden an integration/content agreement.
